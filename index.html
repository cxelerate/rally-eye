Setup tips
  1. Set the table first. With the table set, only a moving ball over it is tracked: balls on the floor, lettering on the table, the net and resting balls are ignored, and the tracker knows how big the ball must look.
  2. If the ball is missed, hold it up in front of the camera, click Pick from video under Ball, and tap it. That fits the colour range to your ball under your light.
  3. Camera at the side of the table, about level with the net, so the whole table fits in the frame. Bounces read best from the side.
  4. Steady light, plain background. Turn off auto-exposure flicker if you can and keep orange or white objects out of view.
  5. Set the table once with the automatic finder or by tapping its four corners. Keep the laptop still afterwards.
  6. Check the mask under Detection: turn on "Show what the tracker sees" and only the ball should light up. If other things glow, raise Saturation or pick the colour again.

Everything runs in your browser. No video leaves your device.
